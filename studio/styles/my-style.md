# AK Consultancy video style (style memory)

Every run reads this file before making a reel, story or carousel. Add to it; never delete approved lines.

## Look
- Background: deep navy radial gradient (#18306e centre -> #050b1e edges), light film grain, soft vignette.
- Accent: gold #f2c14e (3D gold titles with stacked shadow), highlight gold #ffdf8a. Green #25d366 only for the WhatsApp button. Red #ff5a4f only for "galti / mistake" badges.
- Fonts: Montserrat 900 for titles (tight letter spacing about -0.035em), Poppins 500-700 for body.
- Cards: soft glass look done with gradients and borders. Never use backdrop-filter (blurs the whole frame in the renderer).

## Motion
- Measured eases from motionmaxxing (softLand, snapSettle, crashIn, popOver); never linear.
- Words arrive one by one (rise or pop), the key word in gold.
- Big numbers and keywords slam in (crashIn from 2.4x with blur). A white flash on every scene cut.
- Scene length 3 to 3.5 s; nothing stays frozen (slow drift or scale on every scene).
- One 3D hero moment per reel at most (globe + route + plane for travel / study; passport or calendar for USA appointments).
- Progress bar "GALTI 1 / 3" style for list reels.

## Sound
- Soft 120 bpm beat under everything; whoosh landing on every cut; deep impact on hook and CTA slams; pop on badges; rising ticks on progress bars; riser into the CTA.
- Only synthesized or CC0 / CC-BY sounds. Final mix about -14 LUFS, peak -1 dB.
