# AK Consultancy video style (style memory)

Every run reads this file before making a reel, story or carousel. Add to it; never delete approved lines.

## Look
- Background: deep navy radial gradient (#18306e centre -> #050b1e edges), light film grain, soft vignette.
- Accent: gold #f2c14e (3D gold titles with stacked shadow), highlight gold #ffdf8a. Green #25d366 only for the WhatsApp button. Red #ff5a4f only for "galti / mistake" badges.
- Fonts: Montserrat 900 for titles (tight letter spacing about -0.035em), Poppins 500-700 for body.
- Cards: soft glass look done with gradients and borders. Never use backdrop-filter (blurs the whole frame in the renderer).

## Motion
- Measured eases from motionmaxxing (softLand, snapSettle, crashIn, popOver); never linear.
- Words arrive one by one (rise or pop), the key word in gold.
- Big numbers and keywords slam in (crashIn from 2.4x with blur). A white flash on every scene cut.
- Scene length 3 to 3.5 s; nothing stays frozen (slow drift or scale on every scene).
- One 3D hero moment per reel at most (globe + route + plane for travel / study; passport or calendar for USA appointments).
- Progress bar "GALTI 1 / 3" style for list reels.

## Sound
- Soft 120 bpm beat under everything; whoosh landing on every cut; deep impact on hook and CTA slams; pop on badges; rising ticks on progress bars; riser into the CTA.
- Only synthesized or CC0 / CC-BY sounds. Final mix about -14 LUFS, peak -1 dB.

## RULE: REALISTIC IMAGES ONLY (user, 19 Nov 2026) - applies to every video, story and post
The user said our videos look like cartoons. From now on:
- Use photorealistic pictures and footage only: real-looking people (Indian consultants and families, office desk, laptop, passport in hand), Statue of Liberty, New York skyline, US flag waving, India Gate, aeroplane in clouds, passports, boarding passes, calendar on a desk. Natural light, shallow depth of field, like a real photo shoot.
- No cartoon characters, no flat illustrations, no clip-art icons as the main visual, no "AI toy" 3D. Simple line icons are allowed only as small helpers in info rows.
- Movement on photos: slow push-in / pan (Ken Burns), parallax between person and background, real flag wave, plane crossing the frame.
- AK's real poster look (reference posts on @ak_consultancyindia): deep navy (#0d1b3d) + gold or red headline type, big bold condensed headlines ("US VISA", "APPOINTMENT"), white rounded info rows with icons, yellow or red pill buttons, WhatsApp bar at the bottom with +91 97803 10024, akconsultancyindia.com and info@akconsultancyindia.com, "Delhi | Punjab | Haryana". Use the real AK logo (AK with the plane and Lady Justice, "Legal Immigration Experts").
- Sources for realistic images: HeyGen image generation (photoreal prompts, no text inside the picture), the team's own photos, or licensed stock. Never real people without permission, never a real client's documents or names.
- Compliance stays: no "within X days", no success-rate numbers, no "slots paused / embassy" claims, no real appointment confirmations of clients.

## Format B: realistic presenter / course style (user's reference, 19 Nov 2026) - PREFERRED for courses
The user said graphics-only reels do not look realistic enough. Courses and most reels should follow this format:
- A real-looking presenter talking to camera in Hinglish for most of the video: an AI avatar made in HeyGen from a team member's own photo and voice (only with that person's written consent), or HeyGen stock avatar. Medium close-up, plain warm background, natural hand gestures. Always mark it as AI-generated on Instagram.
- Cut to b-roll every 3 to 5 s: screen recordings (website, forms, WhatsApp, app screens), phone mockups, thumbnail cards. Presenter stays visible in a picture-in-picture box or split screen during b-roll.
- Captions: 2 to 4 words at a time, bold uppercase, white text on a dark navy / purple bar, the key word in yellow; centred around 70% down the frame.
- Hook in the first 2 s (presenter + big logo or bold claim card); slow push-in on the presenter; zoom punch on key lines; a red cross on the wrong way, a tick on the right way.
- End: presenter + "Comment KEYWORD for link" in big gold type + "Hum aapko DM karenge".
- Sound: the presenter's voice is the hero; light music bed ducked under the voice; soft whoosh on cuts only (fewer effects than Format A).
- Pipeline: script (ig-reel + humanizer) -> HeyGen avatar video of the presenter -> HyperFrames / motionmaxxing edit (b-roll, captions, PiP, zooms) -> studio-mix-master to -14 LUFS -> studio-frame-review.
